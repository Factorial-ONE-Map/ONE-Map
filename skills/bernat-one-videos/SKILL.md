# Bernat's ONE use-case videos

A series of 10 short Tella videos ("Day 01" to "Day 10") recorded by Bernat. Each shows Factorial ONE (AI) completing a real task inside a Factorial product. Every video exists in up to 6 languages, each with its own playlist.

Use this skill to drop the right video link (and playlist) into a pitch deck, demo follow-up or email, matched to the **products in the deck** and the **language of the audience**.

## How to use when building a deck

1. **Pick the language** from the deck / prospect market:
   - Spain → `es` · France → `fr` · Italy → `it` · Portugal and Brazil → `pt` · DACH (Germany, Austria, Switzerland) → `de` · anything else / international / mixed audience → `en`
   - If the user names a language, that wins over the market.
2. **Pick the videos** whose products match the products or pains in the deck (see the catalog below). Prefer 1–3 videos per deck; do not paste all 10 unless asked.
3. **Place them**: on the slide for that product, add a "See ONE do it" link/button with the video URL. In interactive HTML decks, use a button or clickable card; in .pptx, a hyperlinked text or shape.
4. **Close the deck** with the playlist link for that language ("See all 10 use cases").
5. **Gaps**: if a video is missing in the chosen language, use the English version and tell the user which one fell back. (Days 03 and 04 were re-uploaded in November 2026; their links use the new `vid_…/view` addresses.)
6. Never invent or alter a URL. Copy them exactly from the tables below.

## Catalog: use case → product

| Day | Use case | Main Factorial product(s) | Domain | What ONE does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Branded deck from your data | ONE + Reports / Analytics | ONE (all) | Turns company HR data into a branded presentation |
| 02 | Late arrivals to signed warnings | Time Tracking + Documents / e-signature | Operations | Spots repeated late arrivals and generates warning letters ready to sign |
| 03 | Gender pay gap by role | Compensation / Salary analytics + Reports | Talent | Analyses pay gap by role from salary data |
| 04 | Your payslip explained | Payroll / Payslips (Documents) | Operations / Payroll | Explains an employee's payslip line by line |
| 05 | Find a free meeting room | Spaces / room & desk booking | Operations | Finds and books an available meeting room |
| 06 | Onboarding course from a deck | Trainings (LMS) + Onboarding | Talent | Converts a slide deck into an onboarding training course |
| 07 | Shift changes from a whiteboard photo | Shift Management / Planning | Operations | Reads a photo of a whiteboard rota and updates shifts |
| 08 | Purchase request from a supplier quote | Procurement | Finance | Creates a purchase request from a supplier quote document |
| 09 | Promotion request and promotion policy change | Performance / Career + Compensation + Policies | Talent | Handles a promotion request and updates the promotion policy |
| 10 | Next year's holidays for every workplace | Time Off / Holiday calendars (workplaces) | Operations | Sets next year's public holidays for every workplace |

Quick lookup by domain:
- **Finance**: 08
- **Talent**: 03, 06, 09
- **Operations**: 02, 04, 05, 07, 10
- **ONE / AI generic, analytics, exec audiences**: 01 (plus any of the above as proof points)
